Glance is a native Mac app that shows every repository, worktree and branch in one window. Read each agent's diff as it writes, mark files as viewed, throw away the bad hunks, and hand the rest back to the agent to commit.

−What Glance leaves alone

Agents stay in charge of git.

Glance reads and reviews. Committing, syncing and merging stay with your agents and your command line, where they already work. There's no staging area: the commit prompt, not the index, decides what gets committed.

+Install

Two minutes, one extra click.

Glance is free, so it isn't notarized by Apple (that needs a paid developer account). macOS will ask you to allow it the first time. The app isn't damaged, and every line of its source is on GitHub.

  1. 1
    Download the DMG

    Get Glance-<version>.dmg from the latest release.

  2. 2
    Drag Glance into Applications

    Open the DMG and drop the app onto the Applications folder.

  3. 3
    Open it, then allow it once

    Open Glance from Applications. When macOS blocks it, follow the steps on the right. Then press ⌘O and choose a repository folder.

When macOS says “Glance” Not Opened

  1. Click Done on the warning (not Move to Trash).
  2. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security.
  3. Under Security, find “Glance” was blocked… and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again.

Prefer Terminal? Remove the download quarantine flag instead. It also fixes “Glance is damaged and can't be opened”.

$ xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glance.app

On macOS 15 and later, Control-click › Open no longer gets past this warning. Use Open Anyway.
